PRACTICAL BENEFITS

BUILT AROUND INDOOR CONVENIENCE.

Quieter indoor impact

Soft silent-ball construction is intended to reduce the sharp impact noise associated with conventional inflated balls.

No routine inflation

No pump or needle is required for normal use, reducing day-to-day equipment maintenance.

Soft-touch options

Flocked and fabric-cover surfaces offer a softer hand feel for supervised indoor play.

Multiple buying options

Choose different sizes, colors and samples before planning a larger school or district order.

Silent-ball products are intended for controlled indoor use. They are not protective equipment and should still be used with age-appropriate supervision and normal activity rules.
