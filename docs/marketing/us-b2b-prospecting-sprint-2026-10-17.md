## Outreach prioritization and qualification

1. **Quickest pilot fit:** independent basketball academies, local Y programs, youth clubs/camps, and after-school activity organizations willing to evaluate a sample first. Confirm they actually operate indoor programs; do not assume.
2. **School buyers:** approach PE coordinators, after-school directors or official purchasing team via the school/district's published supplier process. Ask whether direct solicitation is permitted.
3. **Public-sector buying:** follow vendor registration and bidding rules. Never imply government approval or guarantee acceptance of Stripe prepayment.
4. **Distributor outreach:** independently identify sporting-goods wholesalers who already sell to indoor recreation programs; ask for requirements before quoting prices.

Qualification fields for a real prospect: organization; source URL; relevant indoor program evidence; publicly listed business inquiry channel; city/state; role of contact (not private email scraped from an individual); estimated team sizes (unknown until asked); vendor/process requirements; email sent date; response; next action. **All targets are uncontacted until a real send is confirmed.**
