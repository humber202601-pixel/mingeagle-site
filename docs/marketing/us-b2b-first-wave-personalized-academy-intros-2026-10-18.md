## Send gate: do not bypass

- FTC CAN-SPAM applies to B2B marketing email too. Require truthful sender and subject, commercial identification, a valid physical postal mailing address, a functioning opt-out process (honor within 10 business days) and maintained suppression log.
- Kentucky registered-agent service address cannot automatically be presented as an operating or marketing mailing address. No guessed address, identity or consent.
- Verify product/sample availability, payment eligibility, freight and taxes in a reviewed offer. No fake decibel measurements, celebrity endorsements, school approvals, price guarantees or assumption that these coaches buy 10+ units.
- Do not use automated contact-form submissions or mass messaging. Document actual platform send IDs, consent and replies only if a compliant action really happens.
