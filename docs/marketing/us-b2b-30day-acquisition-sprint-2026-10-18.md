## Qualification and reporting

Stages: RESEARCHED -> FIT_VERIFIED -> CHANNEL_VERIFIED -> CONTACTED -> REPLIED -> QUALIFIED_RFQ -> QUOTE_SENT -> SAMPLE_PAID or WHOLESALE_PAID; plus FUTURE, LOST, OPTED_OUT. These are desired reporting stages, **not** existing HubSpot pipeline values; do not create/update CRM schemas without approval. Buyer counts 0 unless actual verification supports each stage.

Scoring: published indoor basketball/activities fit 30; real plausible multi-person training/merch use 25; legal verified business contact route 15; 10+ units if confirmed 15; delivery/procurement fit 15. Unknowns stay UNKNOWN, not inferred. Qualified RFQ requires use case, quantity, destination and reachable buyer; a click or AI mention is not a lead.

## Legal and operating gates

US CAN-SPAM includes B2B commercial emails. Do not send before accurate sender identification, nondeceptive subject, valid postal marketing address, ad identification when applicable, opt-out and a process to honor requests within 10 business days. Follow additional law and platform rules. No automated bulk DMs, fictitious school approval, false sound/acoustic certifications, unverifiable warehouse, invented dB claims or independent reviews. No ad charges without explicit approved spend limit.

Bulk 10+ requires written reviewed quote, shipping terms and Stripe settlement proof. 1–9 samples handled by eligible retail/approved sample flow. China export/US import duties and US state/local sales tax are not the same obligation. Neither the $0.50 owner Stripe checkout nor a PAID database label validates an actual B2B order.
