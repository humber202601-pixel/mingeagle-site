# MING EAGLE Custom Sourcing Page — Internal Release Brief

Date: 2026-11-05

## Purpose
Provide a dedicated route for US schools, districts, institutions, sports organizations and distributors to submit non-catalog sports / PE purchasing requirements.

## Positioning
MING EAGLE is presented as a specialist sports and PE supply partner. Current low-noise indoor balls are the initial product base; other categories are sourcing candidates and are not represented as stocked, pre-approved or immediately available.

## Buyer flow
1. Buyer submits an RFQ, purchasing list or specification.
2. MING EAGLE scopes missing information and intended use.
3. Potential suppliers are identified; factory versus trading intermediary status is checked where evidence is available.
4. SKU-specific specifications, test documents, packaging and traceability are requested and reviewed.
5. Samples and a destination-based written quote are discussed where feasible.
6. Following written acceptance, production follow-up, agreed inspection and shipping documentation are coordinated.

## Categories presented for review
- Sports balls: basketballs, soccer balls, volleyballs, training / activity balls.
- PE and training: cones, marker discs, agility ladders, hurdles, jump ropes, hoops.
- Group activities: activity parachutes, beanbags and soft-play accessories.
- Storage and accessories: ball bags, carts, racks and gym/PE storage.

These categories must be individually screened for supplier capability, product specifications, age grade, safety requirements and buyer acceptance before quotation.

## Commercial and accuracy controls
- Current fulfillment is direct shipment from China; no US warehouse inventory is represented.
- No unsupported decibel reduction, “silent” result, product certification, government vendor status, guaranteed delivery, or universal DDP claim.
- DDP or delivered pricing only after shipment-specific importer-of-record, customs, duty, freight and destination arrangements are confirmed.
- Custom sourcing requests use a separate inquiry type and do not force buyers to select one of the four existing ball SKUs.
- Institutional procurement fields are shown for both wholesale quotes and custom sourcing requests.

## Compliance gate
For each SKU, assess whether it is a children's product, toy, general-use product or another regulated category based on design, marketing, age grading, materials and intended use. Confirm applicable CPSC testing/certification and importer responsibilities before offering regulated products. As of 2026-11-05, CPSC states eFiling of certificates for most regulated consumer products began 2026-07-08; the importer of record / broker must confirm shipment-specific applicability and filing roles.
