# MING EAGLE — US B2B Exposure & Buyer Acquisition Channel Matrix
**As of 2026-10-18.** Companion to the existing [30-day acquisition sprint](us-b2b-30day-acquisition-sprint-2026-10-18.md). This is an actionable opportunity ranking, **not** evidence that any outbound contact, advertising, approval, registration, citation, customer order or revenue occurred.

## Decision rule
MING EAGLE is a US Kentucky LLC selling quiet-style indoor basketball/football products, shipped from China, focused on **10+ units wholesale with reviewed written quotation**; 1–9 eligible units are samples/retail. Product use is controlled indoor activity, NOT a replacement for regulation basketballs; shipping, customs, sales tax, inventory, sample terms and custom logo feasibility require honest SKU/quote-specific confirmation. Stripe is the only merchant checkout/settlement route.

**Priority codes:** P0=first-week buyer outreach research/trial (highest chance of *qualified conversation*, NOT guaranteed sale), P1=implement over next 2–6 weeks, P2=build over 2–6 months, P3=only after gating/strategic fit. **Horizon** is the earliest plausible useful signal, not a promised result. **Cost** is typical incremental cash spend: 0, low (variable) or paid, excluding labor and samples. **Current stage** is opportunity/research unless evidence says otherwise.

## Minimum viable measurement

1. **Sourced organizations researched** count only with operator URL and evidence, not customers.
2. **Channel verified** requires an authorized public supplier inquiry route, not simply any displayed email.
3. **Contacted** requires a real compliant send/submission ID and date; drafts are not contacts.
4. **Replies / qualified RFQs** must be real; qualified RFQ includes model/use, approximate quantity, destination and a reachable procurement contact.
5. **Quotes issued** require merchant-reviewed numbers and approval; **paid orders** require real Stripe settlement proof.
6. **Google indexing** from URL Inspection; **AI citations** from actual observed platform answers/Bing citation report (not synthetic user-agent crawls); **referral visits** by attributable UTM; never mix denominators.
