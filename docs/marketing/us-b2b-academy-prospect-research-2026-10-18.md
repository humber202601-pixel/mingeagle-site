### Evidence/operating gates before a single commercial email

1. **Sender identity and valid physical mailing address:** verify one legally usable company marketing postal address. The Kentucky registered-agent *service-of-process* address is not automatically valid for this purpose.
2. **Reply and opt-out:** configure functioning reply handling, suppression list, working opt-out instructions and a documented process to honor requests within 10 business days. Make solicitation and sender transparent. FTC primary source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
3. **Deliverability:** verify actual sending domain/email authorization (SPF, DKIM and DMARC) if using a company-domain sender; do not claim checks passed from a website alone.
4. **Recipient relevance and platform rules:** prefer a true partnerships/business contact route; do not send vendor pitches into youth registration forms, extract minors' details or falsely imply a partnership.
5. **Product and sample accuracy:** confirm actual unit availability, authorized customization, prices, sample shipping and US sales tax before making offers; the website's Stripe-only checkout and owner $0.50 test are not proof of a completed B2B payment.
6. **Evidence after a send:** log only genuine send ID/time/address channel, opt-out status and later substantive reply; never count draft or research as CONTACTED.
