# MING EAGLE — 14-Day Organic Inbound Publishing Kit

Prepared 10 October 2026. All copy below is ready for review and use; social publishing has not been performed.

## Use this kit

Start with actual MING EAGLE product footage and screen recordings of the approved logo studio. AI can help with captions and editing; it should not simulate product bounce, noise tests, printed samples, testimonials or fulfilled orders. Use creator footage only when its reuse permission covers the channel.

Use 9:16 framing, readable English subtitles and a clear website end frame. Film the product shots in one session, keep original sound for demonstrations, then record the website screens. For a direct sound comparison, keep the same floor, room, microphone position, distance and recording gain; otherwise show the actual ball alone without a numerical reduction claim.

## Review results without adding database traffic

Record platform views, profile visits, link clicks, inbound inquiries and qualified business inquiries in the review table. In HubSpot inquiry details, look for Landing page, Referrer and Campaign. The website’s existing attribution storage happens in the visitor’s session; this kit does not add per-view database writes.

A qualified inquiry should include a real reply address and a relevant buyer role or organization, with useful product, quantity or destination information. Deduplicate repeat requests; do not count demonstrations, spam, individual questions or an existing customer’s support ticket as a new business lead.

Prioritize clips that bring relevant questions and qualified inquiries. If views rise but no one reaches the site, revise the hook and CTA. If visitors arrive but do not inquire, clarify the landing page or sample/quote step before buying traffic. No fixed impression, lead or order target is asserted.
