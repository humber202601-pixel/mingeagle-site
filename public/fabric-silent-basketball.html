How is the fabric-cover set different from the flocked set?

Both are soft non-inflatable indoor ball sets with mini hoops. This model adds a fabric-like cover that changes the surface feel; we do not claim measured acoustic superiority over the flocked model.

What sizes and colors can I choose?

Sizes 3, 5 and 7 are available in orange or green. The set is described as one ball plus one adhesive mini hoop.

Is it silent on apartment floors?

No ball can eliminate footsteps or structural floor vibration. A covered foam ball may reduce sharp impact sounds compared with a conventional inflated ball, but results vary with play style and flooring.
