Silent basketball vs. regular basketball

Choose the ball for the activity: quieter indoor handling and family play have different needs from court practice and competitive games.

MING EAGLE product team · Published 10 October 2026

What changes with foam?

MING EAGLE silent balls use soft, non-inflatable foam. The foam compresses on impact, helping reduce the sharp floor sound. Floor material, room acoustics and playing force still affect what you hear.

A different practice feel

Foam rebound and hand feel differ from an air-filled basketball. Use a sample to judge the ball on your intended floor. For regulation games and drills that depend on game-ball response, use the appropriate conventional basketball.

Choose your MING EAGLE model

For family shooting games, the flocked and fabric-cover sets include a mini hoop. For training-style handling, explore the weighted flocked basketball. The weighted model is ball only; confirm its weight for the size you choose.

Before you buy

Consider the player’s age, intended activity, floor and available space. Review sizes and colors on the product pages. Ask for a sample before a school, academy or retail order, and use the setup guide for mounting and foam preparation.

Is it completely silent?

No. “Silent” describes a quieter foam design, not zero sound. Test in your own room before deciding whether it suits your needs.

Can it replace a game ball?

Treat it as a different indoor-play option. Foam bounce, weight and handling should not be assumed to match a regulation basketball.

Does it need a pump?

MING EAGLE foam balls do not need inflation. Follow the supplied instructions after unpacking, including gentle repeated pressing to help the foam recover.
