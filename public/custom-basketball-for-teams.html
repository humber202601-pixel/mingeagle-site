Custom logo basketballs for teams

Build a team look on an eligible silent basketball. Start with a visual preview, then confirm artwork and production details with your quote.

MING EAGLE product team · Published 10 October 2026

Two eligible basketball models

The Flocked Silent Basketball Set and Weighted Flocked Silent Basketball support the logo studio. Soccer and fabric-cover models are not currently available for logo customization. Select your model, size and ball color first.

Choose text or upload artwork

Text logos are black, except on the black weighted ball, where they are gold. Image logos can keep multiple colors; black areas become gold on the black weighted ball. Check both the artwork and the ball color in your preview.

Prepare your image

Use PNG, JPG or WebP up to 8 MB and 6 megapixels. Transparent PNG is the clearest starting point. Simple solid backgrounds can be removed in the studio; for complex backgrounds, prepare a transparent image first.

Preview, resize and review

Choose a font for text, adjust the logo width and curve, and inspect the front and back views. The larger and smaller controls help you refine the design. Review the design before confirming it.

Save the design reference

After confirming, download the logo artwork as SVG and the design details as JSON. Keep the design reference and files for your records. Designs shown in the current visit are not a substitute for your own saved copy.

Request a custom quote

Send the model, size, color, quantity, destination and design reference. Provide the downloaded artwork for review. Final print size, placement, color and production feasibility are confirmed before production.

Is the preview a final print proof?

No. It is a visual design preview. Artwork and print details still need review and confirmation with your quote.

Can I reuse a design?

Keep your downloaded files and design reference. Provide them with a later order so we can review the same design again.

Can I customize soccer or fabric-cover balls?

Not currently. The studio supports only the two eligible flocked basketball models.

What comes with the basketball?

The Flocked Silent Basketball Set includes a mini hoop. The Weighted Flocked Silent Basketball is ball only. Confirm the order’s packaging and customization scope with your quote.

Custom logo basketballs for school teams and clubs: a practical bulk buying checklist

For school PE programs, basketball clubs and distributors searching for custom team basketballs with a logo, start by confirming the exact ball type and how it will be used. MING EAGLE offers a visual customization studio for selected indoor silent-style basketballs; a preview is not a finished printed sample or a regulation game-ball specification.

Which silent basketballs can carry our school or team logo?

The Flocked Silent Basketball Set (sizes 3, 5 and 7) and Weighted Flocked Silent Basketball (sizes 3, 4, 6 and 7) are the two models supported by the current logo preview studio. Fabric-cover basketballs and silent soccer balls are not currently enabled for this customization tool. Check the actual ball size, color and program suitability before artwork approval.

Can a school request 10, 25, 100 or more customized basketballs?

Requests for 10 or more units enter the wholesale quotation process. Tell us the quantity for each model and size; the final price and any product-specific printing minimum depend on the confirmed supplier requirements, artwork, packaging and order. The website does not advertise an unverified fixed bulk discount or a guaranteed customization minimum.

What school logo artwork can we submit?

The preview studio accepts PNG, JPG and WebP images within its published file limits, including an 8 MB and 6-megapixel limit. A transparent PNG is useful for reviewing placement. Use a school or team logo that you have permission to reproduce, and provide the original approved artwork when requesting a production quotation. Screen previews and colors may differ from a final physical sample.

Is the logo preview an approved printing proof?

No. Save the design reference and downloadable artwork from the studio, then request confirmation of printing method, placement, dimensions, colors, packaging and sample approval in writing. Do not commit a team budget based only on a simulated 3D appearance.

Can a school order one sample before approving a bulk purchase?

Ask about a physical product sample and, separately, whether a customized print sample is feasible and what it costs. Some retail samples may be eligible for air parcel delivery. A plain product sample is not proof that a requested logo treatment or wholesale packaging has been approved. See the school purchase RFQ checklist.

What should a delivered custom basketball quote include?

Ask for itemized product prices, customization and setup, quantity by size, carton or packaging requirements, sample and artwork sign-off, destination ZIP or postal code, shipping route, estimated delivery, import-duty responsibility and applicable sales-tax treatment. For US bulk sea freight, approximately 25–30 days is a planning estimate, not a committed delivery date. Import duties and state sales tax are different charges; the applicable treatment needs confirmation for each order.

Copy this checklist before requesting logo basketballs

Use this brief to reduce back-and-forth with a supplier. A visual logo preview is a planning aid, not proof of a finished printed sample.

Product and use

Name the exact model and size, intended activity and preferred color. Confirm that the chosen surface supports the proposed customization.

Artwork

Provide your own authorized logo, requested colors, placement and approximate print dimensions. Ask which file format is needed.

Quantity and destination

State the number of units and the actual destination country and postal code. Ask for the applicable minimum order quantity.

Sample and approval

Confirm sample costs, artwork approval and whether a physical print sample is needed before a larger run.

Delivered quote

Request item price, customization, freight, import-duty handling, applicable taxes and dispatch/transit estimates in writing. Do not assume a fixed bulk discount.
