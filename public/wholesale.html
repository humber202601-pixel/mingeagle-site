What to include in a useful RFQ

Product/model and size · quantity per SKU · standard or custom branding · destination country/ZIP code · required delivery date · any special packaging needs.

Samples, carton dimensions, logo feasibility, minimum order quantities, delivery times, shipping costs and duties are confirmed case by case. No unverified certification, U.S. stock or guaranteed delivery claim is made here.
