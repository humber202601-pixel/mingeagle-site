BUSINESS POLICIES

CLEAR BEFORE YOU BUY.

This page explains the general framework used for website inquiries and international business orders. Your written quote or order confirmation may contain more specific commercial terms; those specific terms control that transaction.

On this pageQuotes & ordersShippingPaymentReturns & delivery issuesPrivacyCommunications
01

QUOTES & ORDERS

Website forms are requests for information or quotations; submitting a form does not create a binding purchase or charge a payment method. A transaction is confirmed only after the relevant product configuration, quantity, pricing, freight, payment terms and delivery expectations are accepted in writing.

Wholesale pricing is not publicly displayed because quantity, destination, shipping method and customization can materially change the total cost.

02

SHIPPING

Current international orders are quoted based on the delivery destination and the shipment method available for the specific order. Estimated transit times are estimates rather than guarantees and can be affected by carrier operations, customs, weather and destination-country processes.

Where duties, import taxes or local fees may apply, the written quote or shipping arrangement should state how those charges are handled. If the written quote does not make this clear, ask before payment.

03

PAYMENT

Approved orders receive payment instructions or a secure payment link associated with the quote. Do not make payment based only on an unsolicited message or to account details that do not match the agreed transaction.

Public product pages do not collect payment because wholesale orders require a confirmed amount first.

04

RETURNS & DELIVERY ISSUES

If a shipment arrives damaged, incomplete or materially different from the confirmed order, contact us promptly and keep the outer carton, product packaging and relevant photos or video. The available remedy depends on the nature of the issue and the terms confirmed for the order.

Customized products, custom packaging and bulk business orders may have different cancellation or return conditions. These should be confirmed before production or dispatch.

05

PRIVACY

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, manage samples and orders, provide customer support, prevent abuse, and maintain business records related to the request.

Information may be handled through service providers needed for communication, CRM, payments, fulfillment or shipping. We do not publish customer contact information. If you want to correct or remove information you previously submitted, contact mingeaglecommerce@gmail.com.

06

COMMUNICATIONS

Transactional messages related to a quote, sample, order or support request are used to complete the service you requested. Marketing updates are optional and should be sent only when you have opted in or where otherwise permitted.

Questions before paying?

Ask us to clarify anything in the written quote that is unclear — especially freight, duties, customization, production timing or return conditions.
