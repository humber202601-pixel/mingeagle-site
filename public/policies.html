BUSINESS POLICIES

CLEAR BEFORE YOU BUY.

This page explains the general framework used for website inquiries and international business orders. Your written quote or order confirmation may contain more specific commercial terms; those specific terms control that transaction.

On this pageQuotes & ordersShippingPaymentReturns & delivery issuesPrivacyCommunications
01

QUOTES & ORDERS

Website forms are requests for information or quotations; submitting a form does not create a binding purchase or charge a payment method. A transaction is confirmed only after the relevant product configuration, quantity, pricing, freight, payment terms and delivery expectations are accepted in writing.

Wholesale pricing is not publicly displayed because quantity, destination, shipping method and customization can materially change the total cost.

02

SHIPPING

Standard retail products are available to order, with replenishment as needed. Orders dispatch within 3 calendar days of ordering from China. US air parcel delivery is estimated at 12–15 days after dispatch; this transit estimate is separate from the dispatch commitment. For 1–9 P1-S3/P1-S7 sets, each set ships in a separate one-unit parcel; shipping is charged per set, includes import customs clearance and duties, and parcels may arrive separately. Final US sales tax is shown before payment. Consolidated shipments and other products require a confirmed quote. Custom and wholesale order timing follows the confirmed quotation.

Current international orders are quoted based on the delivery destination and the shipment method available for the specific order. Estimated transit times are estimates rather than guarantees and can be affected by carrier operations, customs, weather and destination-country processes.

Where duties, import taxes or local fees may apply, the written quote or shipping arrangement should state how those charges are handled. If the written quote does not make this clear, ask before payment.

03

PAYMENT

MING EAGLE uses Stripe as its online payment provider. A retail order may proceed to secure Stripe checkout only when the final amount and applicable delivery/tax requirements are approved. A wholesale order may proceed to Stripe payment only after both parties accept the written quote and the specific order is enabled for B2B checkout. Never pay from an unsolicited link.

Submitting an inquiry or accepting a wholesale quote does not charge a payment method. If Stripe checkout is not enabled for the approved order, no payment should be attempted; contact MING EAGLE using the listed official channels.

04

RETURNS & DELIVERY ISSUES

Standard retail orders: Contact mingeaglecommerce@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery if your order is damaged, incomplete or materially different from the confirmed order. Include your order reference and clear photos or video. Once the issue is verified, we will arrange a replacement at no extra charge or a refund, including the original delivery charge for a fully defective or incorrect order. We cover any return postage we require for a confirmed quality issue.

Change-of-mind returns: Request authorization within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unused, complete and in their original packaging. The customer pays return postage, including any international return postage. We do not charge a restocking fee. The product price is refunded after receipt and inspection; the original delivery charge is not refunded for a change-of-mind return.

Contact us before shipping any return. We will provide the return address and instructions; do not send returns to the Kentucky registered-agent address. Approved refunds go to the original payment method. We submit approved refunds within 5 business days; your bank or card issuer may take additional time to post them.

Customized products, custom packaging and wholesale business orders follow the specific terms confirmed before payment or production. These policies do not limit rights that applicable consumer law provides.

05

PRIVACY

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, manage samples and orders, provide customer support, prevent abuse, and maintain business records related to the request.

During your visit, the website remembers the landing page, referring page and campaign tags in browser session storage. These details accompany inquiries and prepared email or WhatsApp messages so we can understand which content led to your request. Preparing a message does not send it; you review and send it in your email or WhatsApp app.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregated website traffic and performance, including visits to pages and general referral trends. It is a privacy-focused analytics service and does not use advertising cookies. We do not send website inquiry form contents, customer email addresses, phone numbers, order or payment data to our website analytics script. This measurement does not change your communication choices or marketing-consent preferences. For more information about the provider, see Cloudflare Web Analytics.

We also use Google Analytics 4 only if you choose “Accept analytics” in the optional privacy notice. If you reject optional analytics, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. When enabled, it measures page visits and general referral trends; page URLs are sent without query strings or fragments. For inquiries explicitly accepted by our primary or backup form service, it may also record a generic completed-lead event (such as wholesale or sample request) after you have opted in, without sending the reference or form contents. We do not send inquiry form contents, customer contact details, order details or payment data to Google Analytics. You can change your choice at any time using the Privacy settings control shown on the website. Learn more about Google privacy practices.

Information may be handled through service providers needed for communication, CRM, payments, fulfillment or shipping. We do not publish customer contact information. If you want to correct or remove information you previously submitted, contact mingeaglecommerce@gmail.com.

06

COMMUNICATIONS

Transactional messages related to a quote, sample, order or support request are used to complete the service you requested. Marketing updates are optional and should be sent only when you have opted in or where otherwise permitted.

Questions before paying?

Ask us to clarify anything in the written quote that is unclear — especially freight, duties, customization, production timing or return conditions.
