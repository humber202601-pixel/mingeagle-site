QUIETER PLAY, EXPLAINED.

Quick answer

A silent basketball uses a soft PU foam structure instead of a conventional pressurized rubber bladder. The foam compresses on impact and reduces the sharp floor slap of a regular basketball. It is designed for indoor play — quieter, not literally sound-free.

HOW IT WORKS

WHY DOES IT SOUND QUIETER?

A conventional basketball stores air pressure inside a rubber shell. A silent basketball relies on a soft, porous PU foam body. When it meets the floor, the foam deforms and spreads impact energy over a longer moment, which softens the sharp contact sound.

1

Foam instead of air pressure

The ball does not need inflation. Its cellular PU structure provides shape and rebound.

2

Impact is absorbed

The foam compresses when it hits the floor, helping reduce the hard “slap” associated with conventional balls.

3

Surface changes the feel

Flocked and fabric-cover versions add grip and a softer touch. Weighted versions are built for a more training-oriented hand feel.

Actual sound level and bounce depend on ball model, floor surface, room acoustics and how hard the ball is used. “Silent” describes quieter indoor play, not zero sound.

INSTALLATION

SET UP THE MINI HOOP THE RIGHT WAY.

The adhesive hoop is intended for smooth, clean and stable indoor surfaces. Good surface preparation matters more than pressing harder later.

1. Choose the surface

  • Use a smooth, clean, solid surface such as glass, tile, plastic, wood or metal.
  • Do not mount on plaster, peeling paint, dusty or damp surfaces, unstable wallpaper or weak painted walls.

2. Prepare the adhesive

  • Clean and dry the mounting area first.
  • Remove the protective film from the one-time-use adhesive pad.
  • Place the back panel carefully and press out trapped air.

3. Press and cure

  • Press firmly for at least 1 minute.
  • Wait at least 1 hour before first use for the adhesive to cure.
  • Do not repeatedly remove and reuse the same adhesive pad.

4. Use it as an indoor mini hoop

  • The hoop is approximately 30 cm / 11.8 in across in the supplied instruction reference.
  • Flexible construction helps absorb light impact.
  • It is not designed for aggressive dunking or forceful slamming.
Important mounting note

Adhesive performance depends on the wall material and surface condition. If the surface is painted, plastered, peeling, textured, damp or unstable, do not use the adhesive mount there.

BALL CARE

UNPACK. RESTORE. PLAY.

Soft PU foam can be compressed during packing and transport. A few minutes of simple care helps the ball return to its intended shape and feel.

1

Restore after unpacking

Press and knead the ball repeatedly for about 5 minutes to help restore its roundness and bounce after compressed shipping.

2

Air out a new ball

If there is a slight factory odor, place the ball in a ventilated, sunny area for about 2 hours before use.

3

Clean gently

Use a soft cloth and clean water only. Avoid harsh detergents, solvents and aggressive scrubbing.

4

Small pores are normal

Tiny surface pores can be visible on PU foam products. They are a normal result of the foam structure and do not by themselves indicate a defect.

5

Avoid sharp objects

Keep the ball away from keys, blades, sharp furniture edges, long nails and other objects that can cut or tear the foam.

6

Indoor surfaces are best

Use on clean indoor flooring. Rough concrete, abrasive outdoor courts and sharp debris can accelerate wear.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

WHAT BUYERS USUALLY ASK.

Is a silent basketball completely silent?

No. It is designed to reduce the sharp impact sound of a conventional inflated basketball. Floor type, room acoustics and playing force still create some sound.

Does it need a pump?

No. The PU foam ball is non-inflatable. After shipping compression, kneading and repeated pressing help restore shape and rebound.

Why does a new ball look slightly compressed?

Soft foam can deform during packaging. This is usually temporary. Work the ball by hand for several minutes and allow it time to recover.

Which model feels most like training equipment?

The Weighted Flocked Silent Basketball is the training-first option. It uses a weighted build and a flocked surface for a more substantial hand feel. Exact weight is confirmed by size when quoting.

Can the hoop be attached to any wall?

No. Use only on smooth, stable surfaces. Avoid plaster, peeling or weak paint, textured surfaces, damp walls and unstable wallpaper.

Can the ball or hoop be customized?

Customization depends on product, quantity and artwork. Send your logo, quantity and destination through the inquiry form and we will confirm the available process before production.
