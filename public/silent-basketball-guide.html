THE BASICS

Why does it
sound quieter?

A silent ball uses soft PU foam instead of an inflated air bladder. The foam compresses on impact, softening the sharp floor contact of a conventional basketball.

It needs no pump. Its bounce and feel differ from an inflated match ball, and depend on the model and the floor.

Quieter play still makes some sound. Floor material, room acoustics and playing force all affect what you hear.

02 / FIRST USE & EVERYDAY CARE

Unpack. Recover.
Ready to play.

Soft foam can arrive slightly compressed after transport. Give it time to recover before judging its shape or bounce.

1

Gently restore the shape

Use both hands to gently squeeze, rotate and press the ball for a few minutes. Let the foam recover between presses. Do not pull at the surface or try to inflate it.

2

Wipe, rather than scrub

Lift everyday dust with a soft cloth. If needed, lightly dampen the cloth with clean water. Avoid soaking, strong cleaners and forceful rubbing. Let the ball air-dry.

3

Use a clean indoor floor

Start with light dribbles on a clear indoor surface. Keep sharp edges, grit and rough concrete away from the foam and its cover.

Care illustrations show the flocked basketball. The fabric-cover and soccer models have different outer surfaces; handle their covers gently too.

Still misshapen after recovery?

Stop using a ball with tears or other damage. Send us a photo and your order details so we can help.

03 / ADHESIVE MINI HOOP

A good setup starts
with the surface.

This orange mini hoop comes with the flocked and fabric-cover basketball sets. It is intended for light indoor shooting.

  1. 1

    Check the mounting area

    Choose a smooth, solid, clean and dry indoor surface. Avoid textured walls, plaster, wallpaper, peeling or weak paint, and damp areas.

  2. 2

    Clean, dry and position

    Prepare the surface and plan the height before removing the adhesive backing. Leave enough space around the hoop for light shooting.

  3. 3

    Attach and allow time to bond

    Fit the adhesive mount as directed in the enclosed instructions. Press evenly, and follow the supplied adhesive's waiting time before use. Do not move and reuse the same pad.

  4. 4

    Check before each session

    Confirm that the mount is secure. Use gentle shots with the set's soft ball. Do not hang from the rim, pull the net or use it for forceful dunking.

Adhesive mounts depend on the surface.

If the wall finish or adhesive condition is unsuitable, choose another mounting location. The hoop is not designed to support body weight.

04 / KEEP IT IN GOOD SHAPE

Small habits.
More play.

Care for the foam body and the outer surface together.

01

Give it a place to recover

Store in a dry, ventilated space at room temperature. Avoid prolonged compression, direct heat and extended sunlight.

02

Protect the surface

Avoid pulling flocking or fabric, using sharp objects, or rubbing the ball on abrasive flooring. Small pores are part of the foam structure.

03

Keep play controlled

Clear fragile objects from the play area. Supervise children's play and choose a model and activity suited to the user.

Is a silent ball completely silent?

No. Its foam body softens impact sound, but floor type, room acoustics and the force of play still matter. It is designed for quieter indoor play.

Does the ball need a pump?

No. These PU foam balls are non-inflatable. Gently work a compressed ball by hand and allow it to recover after unpacking.

Is its bounce the same as a regular basketball?

No. Foam balls feel and rebound differently from inflated basketballs. Surface, size and weighted construction also change the experience. Request a sample if a specific training feel matters to you.

Which products include a mini hoop?

The Flocked Silent Basketball Set and Fabric-Cover Silent Basketball Set include the adhesive mini hoop. The Weighted Flocked Silent Basketball and Flocked Silent Soccer Ball are ball models.

Can the hoop attach to any wall?

No. The adhesive needs a suitable smooth, solid, clean and dry surface. Avoid unstable wall finishes, wallpaper, textured walls, plaster and damp areas. Follow the instructions supplied with your set.

Can schools or distributors order samples?

Yes. Send the model, size, color, quantity and destination through our inquiry form. We can discuss samples, bulk pricing and available customization before you order.
