BEFORE YOU INSTALL

Choose the right surface.

Adhesive mounting needs a smooth, clean, solid and dry surface. Check the wall finish before you remove the protective film.

✓

Suitable surfaces

Smooth, stable and clean

GlassTilePlasticWoodMetal
×

Do not use on

Weak finishes or unstable surfaces

  • Painted or plastered walls
  • Peeling surfaces or unstable wallpaper
  • Dusty, damp or textured surfaces
Prepare first.

Clean the mounting area and let it dry fully. Choose the position before applying the one-time-use adhesive pad.

02

HOOP INSTALLATION

Four steps to a secure setup.

Use the adhesive pad supplied with your set. Work in this order, and allow the mount to bond before the first shot.

  1. STEP 01

    Clean & dry

    Clean the chosen smooth, solid surface. Remove dust and moisture, then let the area dry completely.

    Start with a prepared surface
  2. STEP 02

    Peel & apply

    Remove the protective film from the one-time-use adhesive pad. Attach it firmly to the back panel and press repeatedly to remove trapped air.

    Do not reuse the adhesive pad
  3. STEP 03

    Mount & press

    Affix the back panel to the prepared surface and fit the hoop. Press the mount firmly and evenly for at least 1 minute.

    1 minute minimum pressing time
  4. STEP 04

    Wait, then check

    Wait at least 1 hour before use so the adhesive can bond. Check that the panel and hoop are secure before starting light indoor play.

    1 hour minimum before use

Light shooting only.

The flexible hoop can deform slightly on impact. Do not hang from the rim, use forceful dunks or slam the ball against it.

BALL PREPARATION

Get the foam ready for play.

The soft foam body may be compressed during shipping. Prepare it after unpacking, following the ball-care instructions supplied with your set.

AFTER UNPACKING

Press repeatedly
for 5 minutes.

Gently press and release the ball with both hands, rotating it as you work. This helps the compressed foam recover its shape and bounce.

04

CARE & SAFE USE

Keep the surface in good shape.

Soft cloth. Clean water.

Wipe gently with a soft cloth and clean water only. Do not use detergents, harsh cleaners or aggressive scrubbing.

Small pores are normal.

Tiny surface pores can be visible because of the foam structure. They are a normal manufacturing characteristic.

Keep sharp objects away.

Avoid fingernails, keys, sharp furniture edges and other objects that can cut or damage the foam or its cover.

Designed for indoor play.

Use a clean indoor floor and controlled play. Avoid rough outdoor courts, forceful slamming and aggressive dunking.

For the fabric-cover model

Handle the outer fabric gently too. The illustrations show the flocked basketball; the covered and soccer models have different surface finishes.

QUESTIONS & REFERENCE

A little more help.

Is a silent ball completely silent?

No. Soft foam reduces the sharp impact sound of a conventional basketball, but floor material, room acoustics and playing force still affect the sound.

Can I move the hoop to another wall?

The supplied adhesive is for one-time use. Do not remove and reuse the same pad. Check a new mounting surface and contact us if you need a replacement pad.

What if the mount feels loose?

Stop using the hoop. Check whether the surface is clean, dry, smooth and stable, and whether the adhesive has been given the full waiting time. Contact us with a photo for help.

What if the ball is still misshapen?

Repeat gentle pressing and allow the foam to recover. If the ball remains misshapen or has tears or other damage, send us a photo and your order details.
