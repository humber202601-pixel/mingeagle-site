Five ball games that skip repeated floor bouncing

Try short, controlled activities during agreed play hours. Soft balls and mats do not remove footsteps or building vibration; pause if the activity still disturbs other people.

Seated handovers
Sit facing a partner and hand the ball across slowly. Aim for ten careful exchanges without throwing.

Around the waist
Stand in a clear space and pass a held ball around the waist. Change direction after five controlled circles.

Mirror moves
One player makes slow movements with a held ball while the other copies. Switch leader after 30 seconds.

Step and freeze
Carry the ball for three small steps and stop on a signal. Keep the path clear and keep the ball off the floor.

Low target rolls
If the floor surface and household rules allow, sit down and gently roll toward a broad floor target. Skip this game if rolling still carries noise.
