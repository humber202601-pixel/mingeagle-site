BASKETBALLS FOR SCHOOLS: THREE QUESTIONS TO CHECK FIRST.

What basketballs are available for schools?

MING EAGLE lists flocked and fabric-cover silent basketballs in sizes 3, 5 and 7, plus weighted flocked basketballs in sizes 3, 4, 6 and 7. Schools should choose by activity, age group, desired feel and supervision needs.

Can a school request samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Schools can request samples before planning a larger order. Product availability, customization, freight, lead time and final pricing are confirmed in a written quote.

Are silent basketballs completely silent?

No. Soft silent-ball construction is intended to reduce the sharp impact noise of conventional inflated balls, but it does not eliminate footsteps, floor vibration or every sound. Supervision and appropriate indoor activity rules are still needed.

FREE TO READ & SHARE

A no-bounce ball session for a small PE group

A simple 10-minute activity to adapt to your space, pupils and supervision. Use an appropriate soft ball; a MING EAGLE purchase is not required.

2 minutes · Set the space

Clear a small activity zone and agree on a stop signal. Keep the ball in hand: no floor bouncing or wall shots.

2 minutes · Mirror the leader

In pairs, one pupil slowly moves a held ball from waist to shoulder height. The partner mirrors the movement with empty hands. Switch roles.

2 minutes · Careful handovers

Pass hand to hand around a small circle without throwing. Count controlled handovers, then change direction.

2 minutes · Step and freeze

Carry the ball for three small steps, then freeze on the agreed signal. Leave room between pupils and adapt the movement to their abilities.

2 minutes · Review

Ask which movement was easiest to control and which created noise. Reset the area and collect the equipment.

School PE equipment RFQ worksheet: prepare a better bulk quote.

Need a more detailed, copyable form for district procurement? Open the free school basketball bulk-order checklist and full RFQ template, including variant quantities, delivery constraints and buyer documentation.

Purchasing equipment for a school, district, after-school program or youth gym? Use this short checklist to gather the details a supplier needs. It is free to share with a PE teacher or purchasing coordinator and does not require a signup.

1. Who will use it?

Write down age ranges, number of participants, supervised indoor activities and the type of playing surface. A size suggestion is not a safety certification or competition approval.

2. What are the ball requirements?

Choose flocked set, fabric-cover set, weighted flocked basketball or indoor soccer; specify sizes, colors, target quantities, whether the mini-hoop is needed and any branding artwork.

3. What should a sample prove?

Evaluate handling, foam feel, suitability for your room, cleaning and supervised program use. Quiet does not mean soundproof; no independently measured decibel reduction is claimed.

4. What must the quote include?

Share destination state, city, ZIP, desired arrival date, delivery access and any purchase-order requirements. Ask for a written breakdown of items, freight, import duties, applicable sales tax and lead time.
