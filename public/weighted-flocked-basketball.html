What buyers should know about Weighted Flocked Silent Basketball

First-party supplier information about choosing and using this exact model. No independent sound measurements, certification or customer ratings are claimed.

Is the weighted flocked ball the same as a regulation basketball?

No. A weighted-feel soft foam ball is different from a regulation inflated basketball in bounce, grip and handling. Do not assume an identical training experience.

Which sizes and colors are listed?

Sizes 3, 4, 6 and 7 come in orange, brown, black and aqua blue. Exact size-dependent physical weight should be requested and checked before a program order.

Should a school or academy order a sample first?

Yes, evaluating a sample against participants, planned drills, floor type and supervision requirements is a sensible step before agreeing on bulk quantities.
