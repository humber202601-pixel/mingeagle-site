OUR WORKFLOW

From purchasing list to a reviewed quotation.

STEP 01

Share your RFQ

Send product names, specifications, images, quantities, destination and target timing.

STEP 02

Scope the requirement

We identify missing details, applicable use conditions and buyer-specific documentation needs.

STEP 03

Identify suppliers

We screen potential manufacturers and distinguish actual factories from trading intermediaries where evidence is available.

STEP 04

Verify product evidence

We request SKU-level specifications, test reports, packaging details and traceability information for review.

STEP 05

Sample & quote

Where practical, arrange samples and prepare a written quote with confirmed costs, freight and lead-time assumptions.

STEP 06

Order coordination

After written acceptance, coordinate production follow-up, agreed inspection and shipment documentation.

HELP US QUOTE ACCURATELY

What to include in your purchasing request.

  • Product name, photo, link or reference number
  • Required material, dimensions, weight or performance
  • Quantity by SKU, size and color
  • Destination country, state and ZIP / postal code
  • Target delivery date and preferred shipping method
  • Packaging, labeling, logo or barcode requirements
  • Required test reports, certifications or vendor documents
  • Budget range or target unit price, if available

Have an RFQ, tender list or bill of materials?

Send the requirements even if some details are not yet known. We will first review whether the products and supply route are feasible. A request is not an order, and no price, stock, certification or delivery date is confirmed until stated in writing.

TRANSPARENCY

Clear about what is verified — and what is not.

China-origin supply

Current fulfillment is direct shipment from China. Any US inventory, domestic warehouse or local delivery capability must be confirmed separately; none is implied here.

Product compliance

Applicable requirements depend on product design, age grading, materials, intended use and buyer specifications. We will not claim a certificate or test result that has not been verified for the exact SKU.

Freight & import

Freight, customs classification, duties, importer-of-record responsibilities and delivery terms are assessed by shipment. DDP or a delivered price is offered only when the route and responsibilities can be confirmed.

Samples before scale

Where appropriate, we recommend evaluating a sample before a larger commitment. Sample cost, delivery and evaluation criteria are agreed in advance.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Custom sourcing FAQs.

Do you stock every product shown on this page?

No. These are categories we can review for sourcing feasibility. Availability, supplier, specification, price and lead time are confirmed individually.

Can you source items not shown in your current catalog?

Yes, you can submit a request for review. We will assess whether a suitable supplier and compliant supply route can be verified before making an offer.

Can you support a formal school or government tender?

We can review the published specifications and assess whether we can quote. Tender eligibility, registrations, certifications, insurance, bid bonds and other mandatory requirements must be checked against the specific solicitation; no eligibility is implied.

Can you deliver DDP to the US?

Only where the product classification, importer-of-record arrangement, customs, duties and destination delivery can be confirmed for the specific shipment. We will not promise DDP in advance.

Are all products independently tested or certified?

No blanket claim is made. Product-specific evidence and applicable requirements must be reviewed for each SKU and intended market.

Do you have a US warehouse?

Current fulfillment is direct shipment from China. We do not represent that MING EAGLE holds US warehouse inventory.
