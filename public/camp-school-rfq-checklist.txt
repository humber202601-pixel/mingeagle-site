2. PROGRAM AND PRODUCT

   Ball sizes:

3. CUSTOMIZATION (IF APPLICABLE)
   Team / school logo requested? Yes / No
   Proposed placement, artwork file, number of print colors:
   Ask supplier to verify model eligibility, minimum quantity and final proof.
   A digital mockup does NOT prove production feasibility.

4. QUANTITY AND SCHEDULE
   Total estimated units:
   For MING EAGLE: 1-9 units normally follow sample / small-order review;
   10+ units follow wholesale quotation and seller/buyer acceptance.
   Need-by date:
   Preferred delivery method: to be quoted
   For wholesale sea freight, 25-30 days can be a planning estimate where
   the agreed route supports it; it is not a guaranteed delivery commitment.

5. FORMAL PURCHASE TERMS TO CONFIRM IN WRITING
   Product specifications / any required certificates (request evidence)
   Quantities, pricing and available discounts
   Package dimensions and carton quantities
   Shipping, DDP import duty / customs clearance, and destination charges
   Applicable US sales-tax treatment and any valid exemption documentation
   Payment terms and final buyer approval before Stripe payment
   Lead-time assumptions / required delivery dates
   Return and damage-handling terms

6. FIT AND SAFETY CHECK
   Use an appropriate sample with supervision before a group purchase.
   Silent-ball products are not perfectly silent, competition basketballs,
   independently certified noise-reduction devices or safety equipment.
   Ask for verified material, age-use and cleaning information if required.
