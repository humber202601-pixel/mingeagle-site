SCHOOL DISTRICT & INSTITUTIONAL RFQ QUESTIONS

What should a school or public recreation buyer ask before purchasing quieter indoor basketballs in bulk?

Use these questions to compare written quotes, choose products for supervised activities, and avoid confusing product claims with formal purchasing approvals. MING EAGLE supplies product information and quotes; each institution determines whether the equipment and supplier meet its own procurement requirements.

Are soft silent-style basketballs the same as regulation basketballs?

No. Soft, non-inflatable silent-style balls can support controlled indoor ball-handling, mini-hoop play and activity stations. They do not provide regulation rebound or guaranteed acoustic performance. Compare the flocked set, fabric-cover set, and weighted practice model against your program's age, supervision and activity requirements.

What minimum order quantity qualifies for a bulk quotation?

Requests for 10 or more units can enter the wholesale RFQ process. Product size, color, customization, packing, supplier minimums, landed cost and final discounts are reviewed per quotation. Smaller requests can be considered as samples when the relevant model is eligible. See the four-family wholesale product catalog.

Can a school review a sample before committing to a larger order?

Yes, request sample options and confirm the exact product, delivery address, freight, taxes and payment eligibility in writing. For classroom or academy selection, compare fit and handling in a supervised trial rather than relying on an unverified noise-reduction percentage. Use the free school bulk-order checklist.

How are sea freight, import duties and US sales tax handled?

For bulk orders, a written quote states the route, estimated transit, customs responsibilities and pricing for the specified destination. Sea freight can take approximately 25–30 days under the discussed bulk-shipping model, but this is an estimate and not a delivery commitment. Import duties and customs clearance are separate from potential state or local sales tax. Any zero-tax or tax-exempt treatment requires a documented review; it is not automatic.

Can MING EAGLE accept a school or municipal purchase order?

Tell us the agency's purchase-order rules and vendor onboarding requirements before requesting final terms. A quote does not mean that MING EAGLE is registered, approved, or qualified in any government procurement program. Availability of requested documents and acceptable payment arrangements must be confirmed; online order payment uses the approved Stripe route.

What information makes a buyer-ready request for quotation?

Provide the institution and buyer contact, ball family and size, intended activity and age group, quantity, branding or packing requirements, destination ZIP, desired arrival date, required supplier documents and sales-tax exemption information if applicable. Compare with the school buyer guide and silent basketball bulk buying guide.
