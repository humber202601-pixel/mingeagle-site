Custom logo basketballs for school teams and clubs: a practical bulk buying checklist

For school PE programs, basketball clubs and distributors searching for custom team basketballs with a logo, start by confirming the exact ball type and how it will be used. MING EAGLE offers a visual customization studio for selected indoor silent-style basketballs; a preview is not a finished printed sample or a regulation game-ball specification.

Which silent basketballs can carry our school or team logo?

The Flocked Silent Basketball Set (sizes 3, 5 and 7) and Weighted Flocked Silent Basketball (sizes 3, 4, 6 and 7) are the two models supported by the current logo preview studio. Fabric-cover basketballs and silent soccer balls are not currently enabled for this customization tool. Check the actual ball size, color and program suitability before artwork approval.

Can a school request 10, 25, 100 or more customized basketballs?

Requests for 10 or more units enter the wholesale quotation process. Tell us the quantity for each model and size; the final price and any product-specific printing minimum depend on the confirmed supplier requirements, artwork, packaging and order. The website does not advertise an unverified fixed bulk discount or a guaranteed customization minimum.

What school logo artwork can we submit?

The preview studio accepts PNG, JPG and WebP images within its published file limits, including an 8 MB and 6-megapixel limit. A transparent PNG is useful for reviewing placement. Use a school or team logo that you have permission to reproduce, and provide the original approved artwork when requesting a production quotation. Screen previews and colors may differ from a final physical sample.

Is the logo preview an approved printing proof?

No. Save the design reference and downloadable artwork from the studio, then request confirmation of printing method, placement, dimensions, colors, packaging and sample approval in writing. Do not commit a team budget based only on a simulated 3D appearance.

Can a school order one sample before approving a bulk purchase?

Ask about a physical product sample and, separately, whether a customized print sample is feasible and what it costs. Some retail samples may be eligible for air parcel delivery. A plain product sample is not proof that a requested logo treatment or wholesale packaging has been approved. See the school purchase RFQ checklist.

What should a delivered custom basketball quote include?

Ask for itemized product prices, customization and setup, quantity by size, carton or packaging requirements, sample and artwork sign-off, destination ZIP or postal code, shipping route, estimated delivery, import-duty responsibility and applicable sales-tax treatment. For US bulk sea freight, approximately 25–30 days is a planning estimate, not a committed delivery date. Import duties and state sales tax are different charges; the applicable treatment needs confirmation for each order.
